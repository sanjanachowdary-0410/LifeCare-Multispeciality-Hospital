About LifeCare Hospital

25 Years of Trusted Healing & Medical Innovation

Established in 2001 as a dedicated community medical center, LifeCare Multispeciality Hospital has grown into a 350-bed quaternary care institution renowned for clinical precision, ethical medical practice, and compassionate bedside care.

Our campus integrates modular laminar-airflow operation theaters, dedicated Cardiac & Neuro ICUs, and soothing patient recovery suites designed to accelerate healing.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, transparent, and evidence-based medical care with empathy and clinical excellence for every patient.

Our Vision

To be the region’s most trusted healthcare sanctuary, pioneering minimally invasive treatments and preventive wellness.

Patient Care Facilities & Achievements

  • 48-Bed Advanced Critical Care ICU
  • 3-Tesla MRI & 128-Slice CT Imaging
  • Cashless Insurance & TPA Desk

Our Medical Departments

Cardiology

Comprehensive heart care including interventional cardiology, echocardiography, electrophysiology, and preventive cardiac rehabilitation.

Neurology

Orthopedics

Minimally invasive joint replacement, arthroscopic sports injury repair, spine surgery, and personalized physiotherapy recovery.

Pediatrics

Gentle, child-friendly care covering newborn neonatology, immunization programs, adolescent wellness, and pediatric nutrition.

Dermatology

Clinical skin, hair, and nail treatments including allergy testing, acne & eczema therapy, and safe laser dermatology procedures.

Gynecology

Holistic women’s healthcare offering high-risk maternity suites, painless birthing, laparoscopic gynecology, and fertility care.

Dentistry

Painless root canal therapy, dental implants, invisible orthodontics, and preventive oral hygiene care for adults and children.

Ophthalmology

Micro-incision cataract surgery, LASIK vision correction, glaucoma screening, and diabetic retinal care using OCT diagnostics.

General Medicine

Primary adult care for diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, infectious fevers, and comprehensive preventive health screenings.

Emergency Medicine

24/7 rapid-response trauma center, cardiac resuscitation bays, poison control, and GPS-enabled ACLS ambulance fleet.

Health & Wellness Hub

Preventive Health Tips & Patient Guidance

Evidence-based wellness advice from LifeCare specialists alongside an interactive BMI health check tool.

Cardiology

Protect Your Heart Daily

Aim for 30 minutes of brisk walking five days a week, limit sodium intake, and monitor blood pressure regularly after age 35.

Preventive Care

Annual Full-Body Screenings

Routine blood sugar, lipid profile, thyroid, and vitamin D checks detect silent metabolic conditions years before symptoms appear.

Nutrition

Balanced Hydration & Diet

Fill half your plate with colorful vegetables, prioritize lean proteins and whole grains, and drink 2.5–3 liters of clean water daily.

Neurology & Sleep

Restorative Sleep Hygiene

Maintain a consistent 7–8 hour sleep schedule and avoid screens 45 minutes before bed to support memory and immune resilience.
