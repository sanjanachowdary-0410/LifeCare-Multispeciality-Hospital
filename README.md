# LifeCare Multispeciality Hospital

A responsive, modern healthcare web application for **LifeCare Multispeciality Hospital**, designed with a clean clinical palette of white, light blue, teal, and soft green, paired with clear typography, interactive department/doctor browsing, a health wellness check, and an appointment request workflow.

**Live Demo:** [https://sanjanachowdary-0410.github.io/LifeCare-Multispeciality-Hospital/](https://sanjanachowdary-0410.github.io/LifeCare-Multispeciality-Hospital/)

---

## Features & Sections

1. **Top Utility Bar & Navigation Header**
   - Displays clinic address, OPD and 24/7 emergency hours, and a quick emergency contact link.
   - Responsive navigation bar with smooth section scrolling and mobile drawer menu covering **Home**, **About**, **Departments**, **Doctors**, **Services**, **Health Tips**, **Contact**, and **Book Appointment**.

2. **Home / Hero Section**
   - Features the hospital headline (*"Compassionate Care, Advanced Medicine"*), tagline (*"Your Health, Our Lifelong Commitment"*), accreditation badge, introduction, **Book an Appointment** and **Emergency Contact** actions, quick links to **Departments**, **Doctors**, and **Appointments**, and a **Patient Care Desk** panel.

3. **Hospital Information & Achievements**
   - Highlights key milestones (`25+` years of clinical excellence, `120+` specialist doctors, `10+` medical departments, `50,000+` patients treated annually), hospital history, **Mission** and **Vision**, and patient care facilities (ICU, 3-Tesla MRI & 128-Slice CT imaging, cashless insurance/TPA desk, and recovery suites).

4. **Medical Departments**
   - Showcases **10 medical departments** with photography, icons, descriptions, and detail views:
     - **Cardiology**, **Neurology**, **Orthopedics**, **Pediatrics**, **Dermatology**
     - **Gynecology**, **Dentistry**, **Ophthalmology**, **General Medicine**, **Emergency Medicine**

5. **Doctor Profiles**
   - Filterable specialist directory featuring doctor photographs, medical qualifications, specializations, years of experience, consultation timings, and **Book Appointment** buttons that pre-select the chosen doctor and department.

6. **Medical Services**
   - Covers core hospital and diagnostic services with icons and descriptions: **General Health Check-ups**, **Laboratory Testing**, **X-ray and Scanning**, **MRI and CT Scan**, **Pharmacy Services**, **Ambulance Services**, **Emergency Care**, **Surgical Services**, and **Preventive Healthcare**.

7. **Health Tips & Wellness Check**
   - Provides preventive wellness guidance and an interactive **BMI (Body Mass Index) Calculator** to help visitors check baseline weight status.

8. **Interactive Appointment Booking**
   - Collects **Patient's Full Name**, **Age**, **Gender**, **Phone Number**, **Email Address**, **Medical Department**, **Preferred Doctor**, **Appointment Date**, **Preferred Time**, and **Reason for Consultation**.
   - Includes form validation, a confirmation summary upon submission, and a clear notice that submitted appointments remain **requests until confirmed by the hospital**.

9. **Contact Information & Footer**
   - Includes hospital location details, phone and email contacts, 24/7 emergency access, social media links, an embedded map, quick navigation links, and copyright information.

## Built With

- **HTML5** — Semantic structure, accessible forms, and interactive modals/panels
- **CSS3** — CSS custom properties (variables), CSS Grid & Flexbox layouts, responsive media queries, hover transitions, and scroll animations
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)** — Mobile navigation toggle, specialty filtering, department detail modals, doctor/department auto-selection, BMI wellness calculator, and appointment form validation
- **Google Fonts & Font Awesome** — Medical/editorial typography and healthcare iconography
